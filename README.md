## Overview

**Formative Reappraisal** is an emotion-regulation approach developed by Timothy J. Xeriland to address disengagement and support re-engagement in learning environments.

The framework originated in doctoral research examining how intelligent tutoring systems could recognize student disengagement and respond with interventions designed to help learners return to productive learning activities.

Formative Reappraisal draws on psychological theories of emotion regulation and applies them to the challenges of learning, motivation, and engagement.

## Origins and Development

Formative Reappraisal was developed and investigated as part of my doctoral research, completed in 2018.

**Doctoral Dissertation**

Xeriland, T. J. (2018). *Intelligent Tutor Systems Addressing Student Disengagement: Adding Formative Reappraisal to Enhance Engagement and Learning.*

The dissertation investigated the incorporation of Formative Reappraisal into intelligent tutoring systems as an approach to addressing student disengagement.

The framework continues to inform my research interests in emotion regulation, human–AI interaction, and adaptive intelligent systems.

## Theoretical Foundations

Formative Reappraisal brings together several related areas of psychological and educational research:

- **Emotion Regulation:** Understanding and influencing emotional responses to challenging experiences.
- **Cognitive Reappraisal:** Reconsidering the meaning or interpretation of an experience.
- **Student Disengagement:** Examining the cognitive, emotional, and behavioral processes associated with withdrawal from learning.
- **Adaptive Intervention:** Developing responses that address disengagement in ways appropriate to the learner and situation.
- **Re-Engagement:** Supporting a return to productive learning following difficulty or disengagement.

The framework is particularly relevant to the development of intelligent systems capable of responding to the emotional and cognitive dimensions of learning.
